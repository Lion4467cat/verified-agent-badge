UNCLONE — Calibration

Where every threshold came from, what was measured, and what is still assumed. Raw version: CALIBRATION.md

Vendor match threshold

0.53VENDORED
DetectifAI's own published match threshold, read back from its response field. Not ours to tune — auto-approval just needs margin above it.

Auto-approve margin

0.75TUNED
Vendor + 0.22. Chosen so staged genuine runs (0.81–0.99) always clear it and a phone-line same-speaker (0.813) still proceeds. Thin identity never auto-approves.

Segment-hold line

0.90TUNED
Tuned on the staged subtle hijack: aggregate "real" (0.337) with hot segments ≥ 0.99. Lower would hold clean calls; higher would miss the hijack.

Forensics artifact line

0.85TUNED
Pure digital silence scores 1.0; real room-tone recordings ≤ 0.51. Above 0.85, audio cannot be an acoustic capture — escalation evidence only, never proof of fraud.

Separation transfers to the second transform family: min clone 0.998 vs max genuine 0.021, and the telephony-band render — the realistic attacker — still scored 0.998, so band-limiting makes synthetic artefacts more detectable, not less. The converted-genuine windows matched identity (0.548/0.742) but fell below our 0.75 auto-approve line, so the margin rule correctly refused auto-approval. Second family ≠ a second ASVspoof-class engine, and FAR/FRR vs unseen real speakers is still open — the honest phrasing is “tuned and transfer-checked within our attack classes”, not “validated”.
