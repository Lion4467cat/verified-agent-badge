These calls are already flagged above the account limit — so 52 % precision means ~1 false step-up per real catch, survivable for a fraud team. Cold-call screening of all inbound calls at the same rates would show ≈ 7 % precision — that contrast is the design thesis: screen flagged traffic, not the firehose.
